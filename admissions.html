Admissions 2026–27

Begin your admission enquiry for Classes 1st to 8th.

Admissions Open · 2026–27

Take the next step with Bright Future School

Admission enquiries are invited for Classes 1st to 8th. Please contact the school to confirm seat availability, eligibility, applicable dates and the required documents.

Admission Process

  1. Submit the admission enquiry form below or contact the school.
  2. Speak with the school about class availability and eligibility.
  3. Visit the school and complete the official application process.
  4. Submit documents requested by the school.
  5. Confirm admission and any applicable fee details directly with the school.

Documents to keep ready

  • Student date-of-birth proof
  • Previous school record, if applicable
  • Parent/guardian contact details
  • Address proof and photographs, if requested
This is a general preparation list, not the official final document checklist. Confirm requirements with the school.

RTE 25% Admission Information

RTE admissions are governed by applicable government rules, eligibility criteria, schedules and official procedures. Contact the school and consult the relevant Rajasthan government notification/portal for current details. This website does not process RTE applications.
